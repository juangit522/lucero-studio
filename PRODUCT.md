# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- **Emprendedores y pymes** that want a custom mobile (Android) or web solution built for them and need to trust a small studio with it.
- **Usuarios de las apps propias** of LUCERO STUDIO who arrive looking for the studio's apps on Google Play.

## Product Purpose

Single-page site for LUCERO STUDIO, a small studio that designs and develops mobile apps (Android native) and web solutions, both for clients and as its own products. Success: a prospective client sends a message through the contact form; an app user finds and downloads the studio's apps.

## Positioning

Trato directo y desarrollo personalizado: the client talks with the people who design and build, and every solution is made to measure, focused on mobile and web.

## Operating Context

- Contact goes through a form posting to Web3Forms (`script.js`), with honeypot, minimum fill time, cooldown and optional CAPTCHA.
- Apps are published on Google Play. None are published yet; the Proyectos section shows a "próximamente" state and has a commented template for real app cards.
- Spanish-language site (`lang="es"`), plus `privacidad.html` (privacy policy) sharing `styles.css`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no build step. Keep `script.js` form IDs and field names intact.
- Services offered: desarrollo de apps Android, diseño UI/UX móvil, publicación y mantenimiento en Google Play.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: LUCERO STUDIO.
- Logo `logo.jpg`: white three-headed winged dragon inside a deep-blue circle with a serif "LUCERO STUDIO" wordmark. Mandatory.
- Blue tones are mandatory for the identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- `logo.jpg` (1024×1024).
- No published apps, clients, testimonials, case studies or metrics yet. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Make the direct, personal relationship tangible: a real studio, not an agency façade.
2. Serve both audiences without forcing either through the other's path.
3. Be honest about stage: no invented proof, "próximamente" stays truthful.
4. Contact must be the easiest action on the page.
